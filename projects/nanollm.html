Tiny Shakespeare experimental dataset

Measured result

On Tiny Shakespeare, the modern Transformer achieved the lowest validation bits per byte at 2.1052, compared with 2.4384 for the vanilla RNN. GRU and LSTM narrowed most of the gap, while the larger LSTM underperformed the smaller one despite using roughly four times as many parameters.
