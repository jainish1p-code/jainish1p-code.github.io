Project 03 · Computer vision · Evaluation

What happens when detectors leave their home dataset?

This benchmark compares three object-detection families on COCO and KITTI to study cross-dataset generalization and the speed–accuracy tradeoff in driving scenes.

View code and report ↗

The comparison

Rather than asking which model is universally best, the benchmark asks how accuracy and speed change when the same detectors face a different visual world and a matched vehicle/person taxonomy.

What the results show

Faster R-CNN led the shared vehicle/person evaluation with AP 0.5731 on COCO and 0.3962 on KITTI. YOLO11s reached 84.82 FPS on native COCO—5.44× the Faster R-CNN throughput—with only a 1.43-point AP difference. Across models, person AP fell by 26–32 percentage points when moving from COCO to KITTI.
